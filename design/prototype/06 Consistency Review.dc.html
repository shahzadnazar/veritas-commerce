05

Handoff — what a developer can build from this

Specified without guessing
  • Every token: colour roles and ramps, type scale, spacing, radius (0), three shadows.
  • Layout metrics: containers, 12-col grid, 240px sidebar, header heights, three breakpoints.
  • Every Phase 1 status and which of four semantic fills it maps to.
  • Component states: default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error.
  • Page states: loading skeletons, empty, error, success — per screen, not generic.
  • Validation rules and their copy (ZIP, compare-at price, payout minimum, required reasons).
  • Which actions demand a reason and where that reason surfaces afterwards.
Business rules made visible
  • Commission snapshot: shown on seller order detail, admin order detail, the ledger and commission settings.
  • Refund reverses earning and commission at the order's stored rate, as new rows.
  • Stock: on hand vs held vs available; movements append-only with actor and reason.
  • Payouts: one open request, minimum $50, held balance, rejection releases and records.
  • Seller can't mark Shipped without a tracking number.
  • Suspension hides listings and freezes balance but never cancels open orders.
Still needs a decision
  • Multi-seller order numbering — one MH- number with sub-orders, or a number per seller.
  • Guest checkout account-claim flow after the order email.
  • Tax calculation source — flat rate per state or a provider.
  • Whether product review is on by default at launch (the settings toggle allows either).
  • Email templates themselves — the matrix of sends is specified, the artwork is not.
  • Return/RMA flow beyond "Request a return" — out of Phase 1 scope as written.
